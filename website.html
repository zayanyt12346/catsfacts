Welcome

Cats are one of the most beloved pets around the world. Known for their agility, independence, and sometimes mischievous behavior, cats have been companions to humans for thousands of years. From their unique ways of communication to their impressive hunting skills, there's so much more to cats than meets the eye.

Interesting Cat Facts

Whisker Sensitivity

Cats use their whiskers to detect changes in the air and to sense nearby objects — even in complete darkness.

Purring Mystery

While cats purr when they’re happy, they also purr when they're scared or injured as a self-healing mechanism.

Flexible Bodies

Cats have a flexible spine and lack a collarbone, which allows them to fit through spaces as small as their heads.

Territorial Creatures

Most cats are very territorial and mark their area with scent glands on their face, paws, and tail base.

Unique Prints

Each cat has a unique nose print — similar to human fingerprints!

Superb Hunters

Even domesticated cats retain hunting instincts. They can stalk, pounce, and catch prey with incredible precision.

Night Vision

Cats can see in light levels six times lower than humans, helping them hunt in the dark.

Sleeping Champions

Cats sleep for 13 to 16 hours a day — that's almost two-thirds of their lives!

High Jumpers

A cat can jump up to six times its body length in one leap thanks to its powerful back legs.
