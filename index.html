Curious Cat Facts

Facts Images Sources
Cats can rotate their ears 180 degrees.
A group of cats is called a clowder.
Cats sleep for 70% of their lives.
The oldest cat lived to be 38 years old.
Cats can make over 100 different sounds.
They have powerful night vision and can see in almost total darkness.
A cat's nose is as unique as a human fingerprint.
Cats walk like camels and giraffes — both right feet then both left feet.
Adult cats only meow to communicate with humans, not each other.
